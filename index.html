ABOUT D’LIFE UNSCRIPTED

Our Story

D’LIFE UNSCRIPTED was created to provide a welcoming space for authentic conversations and shared experiences. We believe that behind every person is a story worth telling. Whether it's about love, family, friendship, career, personal growth, mental wellness, or everyday life, every conversation has the power to inspire and connect. Our goal is to build a community where people feel heard, respected, and understood.

To create a safe and welcoming community where people can share real stories, exchange honest thoughts, and build meaningful connections through conversation.

Our Vision

To become one of the most trusted online communities for storytelling, personal growth, and meaningful conversations.

Every Story Matters

Every person has a story worth telling.

Whether it's a life lesson, a relationship, a personal struggle, a dream, a moment of joy, or a challenge you've overcome, your story has the power to inspire, comfort, and connect with others.

Because real life has no script.

🔒 Your Privacy Matters

Your comfort and privacy are important to us.

When submitting your story, you can choose to:

  • ✅ Share your name
  • ✅ Use a nickname
  • ✅ Remain completely anonymous

If your story is featured in a D'LIFE UNSCRIPTED episode or discussion, we will respect the privacy option you choose.

📋 Submission Guidelines

To help us create a safe and welcoming community, please:

  • ✅ Be honest and respectful.
  • ✅ Avoid sharing private information about yourself or others.
  • ✅ Do not include offensive, hateful, or illegal content.
  • ✅ Submit only stories that you have the right to share.
  • ✅ Understand that not every submission may be featured.

Our goal is to create conversations that inspire, encourage, and connect people.

What Happens After You Submit?

  1. Our team reviews your submission.
  2. Selected stories may be featured in future D'LIFE UNSCRIPTED conversations, live streams, or podcast episodes.
  3. If your story is selected, we'll honor your chosen privacy preference (name, nickname, or anonymous).

Every submission is appreciated, whether or not it is featured.
